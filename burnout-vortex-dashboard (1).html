The Atelier Haus Burnout Vortex™

A live mirror of the Burnout Transit Map™ · Modern Burnout Load Equation™ · Burnout Cycle Equation™

Burnout doesn't rise in a straight line. It behaves like a vortex: demands, fragmentation, micro-stress, body load, isolation, uncertainty, identity loss and meaning deficit all spiralling around a nervous system that's running out of fuel.

This dashboard translates that experience into structure. Each slider represents a line on the Burnout Transit Map™. As you move them, you'll see your current Burnout Load and how that load could evolve over the next few steps if nothing changes. It's not a diagnosis — it's a structural mirror.

Important: This dashboard is for educational and reflective purposes only. It does not diagnose burnout, mental illness, or medical conditions, and is not a substitute for professional medical, psychological, or crisis support. If your distress is high or you are worried about your safety or wellbeing, please seek help from a qualified professional.

05

Intervention & Actionable Levers

Play with the sliders and notice what happens to your Load score and trajectory. You don't have to fix everything at once — start with the levers that feel most possible this week.

◈
Lower Load fast: Increase R (Recovery) — it divides the entire equation.
◈
Stop the spiral: Reduce chronic conditions that feed α — toxic workload, constant crises.
◈
Strengthen damping: Improve β via real rest, sleep quality, nervous system support.
◈
Reduce futility: Increase A (Agency) — small, real choices that reconnect you to your own say.
